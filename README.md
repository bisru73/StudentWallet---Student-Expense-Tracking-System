## I. Problem Statement
Students often have difficulty managing their money and tracking their daily expenses. They spend money on different things, such as food, transportation, internet, and educational materials, but they may not record how much they spend. As a result, they can lose track of their money, exceed their budgets, and struggle to understand where their money goes. This makes it difficult for students to manage their finances effectively and save money for future needs.

---

## II. Solution Overview
StudentWallet is a simple web application designed for university students to manage their personal finances, record income and daily expenses, and gain insight into their spending habits.

### Key Features
* 👤 User Accounts: Student registration and login to manage personal financial information.
* 💳 Income & Expense Tracking: Record daily income sources and expenses easily.
* 🏷️ Expense Categorization: Organize spending into categories (Food, Transportation, Internet, Educational Materials, etc.).
* 🧮 Balance Calculation: Automatically computes remaining balance ($Total\ Income - Total\ Expenses$).
* 📊 Monthly Summary: Overview of monthly total income, expenses, and remaining balance.
* 🎯 Budget Management: Set budget limits and track spending to prevent overspending.
